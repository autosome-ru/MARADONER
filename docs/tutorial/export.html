Exporting results

Exporting tables

The project files of MARADONER are not meant to be read by other software. The export command turns them into plain tables:

> maradoner export MyProject results

Here, results is the output folder. All tables in it are tab-separated, and the first column holds row names. The command runs after predict.

Besides the tables, the folder gets a README.md file. It describes every file found in the folder and every column of it, with respect to the options the tables were made with. The other commands writing into the same folder (contrast, anova, grn and so on) add their files to it. This page of the tutorial is therefore a guide to the folder, and the generated file is the reference.

What is in the folder?

Folder Content Details
activities Deviations of motif activities from their means in each group, their standard deviations and z-scores Motif activities
params Parameter estimates with standard errors Below
tests Tests for each motif Tests
fov Goodness-of-fit statistics, present if gof was run before the export Goodness of fit

Motifs appear in the tables of activities and in most of the tests only if predict kept them.

Parameter estimates

File Rows Content
motif_means.tsv Motifs The mean activity of each motif (mean) and its standard error (std)
motif_variances.tsv Motifs The variance of activity deviations per motif (tau) and its standard error (std)
filtered_motif_variances.tsv Motifs The motifs left out by predict for a negligible variance
group_variances.tsv Groups The error variance (sigma) and the multiplier of activity variances (nu) with their standard errors
promoter_means.tsv Promoters Promoter-wise means
sample_means.tsv Samples Sample-wise means
covariates/intercept.tsv Motifs The mean activity once more, with a two-sided test against zero: effect, std, z, pvalue, fdr
covariates/{covariate}.tsv Motifs For a project with covariates: the change of motif activity per unit of the covariate, with the same columns
factors.tsv Samples For a fit with --activity-lowrank: the factors, a column per factor. error_factors.tsv is the same for --error-lowrank
family_variances.tsv Families For a fit with --motif-families: the variance shared by the motifs of each family (omega), its standard error (std) and the number of motifs (motifs). The tau of a motif is then the variance of its individual part
family_factors.tsv Families For a fit with --motif-families and --motif-lowrank: the factors of the covariance between families, a column per factor. Without families the factors are in motif_factors.tsv, a row per motif
family_correlations.tsv Families For a fit with --motif-lowrank and families: the correlations between families implied by $Ω + L L^T$
activity_correlations.tsv Motifs For a fit with --motif-families or --motif-lowrank: the correlations between the activities of motifs implied by the fitted covariance between motifs
correlations/ Correlations between the estimates of a kind, e.g. between the mean activities of different motifs

The variance of the activity of motif $k$ in group $g$ is the product $τ_k ν_g$. A product does not change if one factor is multiplied and the other is divided by the same number, so the scale of nu is arbitrary. MARADONER settles it by fixing the nu of one group. The standard error of that nu is left empty.

Optional tables

Some tables are large and are written on request only:

Name Description
--log-counts The expression predicted by the full model is written to the log_counts folder: one column per group, or one per sample with --no-log-counts-grouped
--loadings-product For each group, a table of promoters by motifs is written to the loadings-product folder: the loading times the activity (the mean plus the deviation), i.e. the contribution of a motif to the expression of a promoter. The tables are in the HDF format, and --no-lp-hdf switches to plain text. With --no-lp-intercepts the mean activity is left out

A few more options change the content of the regular tables:

Name Description
--weighted-zscore The overall column of z_score.tsv becomes the absolute z-score of the mean of the group activities weighted by inverse variances
--zero-margin <float> Turns the zero column of the sign test into an equivalence test
--anova-mode <str> Chooses the tail of the ANOVA statistic
